How to use it

  1. Type a frequency or use the slider.
  2. Choose sine, square, triangle or sawtooth.
  3. Use sweep to glide between two frequencies.
  4. Start at low volume, especially with headphones.

Questions

What can I use it for?

Testing speakers and headphones, checking your hearing range, tuning, science class demos and finding rattles in a car or room.

Which wave should I pick?

Sine is the purest tone. Square and sawtooth sound buzzier because they contain many overtones.

Why can’t I hear very high tones?

Hearing range drops with age, and small speakers struggle above 18 kHz.
