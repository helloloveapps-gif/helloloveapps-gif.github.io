Netsu Ramen

Heat 8/10 · Hell estimate
Area
Hongje-dong, Seodaemun-gu, Seoul
Address
서울 서대문구 통일로39나길 1 1층
Signature dish
Geukgang Netsu Tonkotsu Ramen (extra-hot tonkotsu), spicy jumbo tonkotsu ramen
Heat levels
Two challenge dishes: the extra-hot Geukgang Netsu ramen and a 4-serving jumbo ramen. Finish everything including the broth in 20 minutes to win (cooling time is free). Fail and you pay 30,000 won, plus 10,000 won if you throw up. Fail money goes to a child welfare fund; limited per day.
Hours
11:00~20:50, 쉬는 시간 15:00~17:00, 금 휴무
Phone
02-2138-0400

Hot ramen is the house specialty (netsu means heat), and the hottest bowl is run as a timed challenge with a penalty. Many challenge YouTubers have visited.
