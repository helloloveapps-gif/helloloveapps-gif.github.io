Privacy Policy — Tone Generator

Last updated: October 19, 2026 · Developer: LoveApps

What the app does

Tone Generator (tone generator, white / pink / brown noise and a hearing range check) creates all sounds on your device. It works offline. It does not use your microphone, camera, contacts, photos or location.

Data we collect

We do not ask for, collect or store personal information on our servers. Your settings (such as the last frequency, saved favorites and language) are saved only on your device.

Hearing range check

The hearing range check is for fun and is not a medical test. Your answers and results are shown on the screen only. They are not saved and are never sent to us or anyone else.

Advertising

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect device information such as the advertising ID, IP address, app interactions and approximate location, and may use cookies or similar technologies. How Google uses this data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

  • In the EEA, UK and Switzerland we ask for your consent before personalized ads. You can change your choice any time from the app menu (“Privacy options”).
  • On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in the system settings.

Purchases

If you buy “Remove ads”, the payment is handled by Google Play. We do not receive your payment details.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes and contact

We will post changes on this page. Questions: hello.loveapps@gmail.com
