Yoon Ok-yeon Halmae Tteokbokki

Heat 6/10 · Fire estimate
Area
Sincheon-dong, Suseong-gu, Daegu
Address
대구광역시 수성구 들안로77길 11
Signature dish
Tteokbokki with fried fish cake and fried dumplings (the '1,000-won trio')
Heat levels
No levels (one flavor). You can ladle on extra sauce yourself
Hours
10:00~20:30, 월요일 휴무(출처에 따라 2·4·5째 월)
Phone
053-756-7597

Opened in 1976 and regarded as the origin of Daegu's spicy tteokbokki. Not sweet at all; the heat comes from black pepper and chili powder, earning it the nickname 'addictive tteokbokki'.
